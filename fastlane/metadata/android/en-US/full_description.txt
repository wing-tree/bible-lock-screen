God's Word, every time you turn on your phone.
The Bible in You shows a Bible verse on your lock screen, so the moments you pick up your phone become moments of reflection on Scripture.

■ Bible verses on your lock screen
• See a Bible verse as soon as you turn on your screen.
• Choose how verses appear:
  - Verse of the day: one verse a day from 432 hand-picked passages
  - In order: read through the whole Bible, Genesis to Revelation
  - Favorites only: cycle through the verses you saved
• Jump to any book, chapter and verse right from the lock screen.
• Turn the lock screen on or off at any time.

■ Read the Bible
• 7 translations including the King James Version (KJV), American Standard Version (ASV), Louis Segond and Luther
• Parallel Bible: read two translations side by side
• Highlight, bookmark and favorite the verses that speak to you
• Works offline: the whole Bible is on your phone, no internet needed

■ Share the Word
• Turn any verse into a beautiful verse image and share it with friends and family
• Copy or share verses as text

■ Bible search
• Search the whole Bible by word
• Search right from the lock screen

■ Comfortable reading
• Dark mode
• Adjustable font size and text alignment

■ Translations
King James Version (KJV), American King James Version (AKJV), American Standard Version (ASV), Updated King James Version (UKJV), Korean Revised Version, Louis Segond 1910 (French), Luther 1912 (German)

■ Permission
• Display over other apps: needed to show verses on the lock screen.

May God's Word be with you every day.
