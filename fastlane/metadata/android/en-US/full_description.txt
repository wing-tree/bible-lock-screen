Lock screen
Every time you turn on your smartphone, you can see the Word of God.
You can turn the feature displayed on the lock screen on or off at any time. :)

Bible
Various translations have been added,
You can view two translations at the same time.
You can remember your favorite sayings by providing highlights, bookmarks and favorites.
Spread the grace to those around you by copying and sharing your favorite words. :)

Search
You can easily find the phrase you want on the lock screen.
You can find verses by searching by Word.

Settings
Support dark mode.
You can freely change the font size.
You can align the text to any position you want.

Permission
Appear on top: Required to run the app on the lock screen.
